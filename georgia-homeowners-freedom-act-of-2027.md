A BILL TO BE ENTITLED AN ACT

To amend Article 6 of Chapter 3 of Title 44 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to property owners' associations, so as to enact the "Georgia Homeowner's Freedom Act of 2027"; to require property owners' associations to establish and maintain secure online portals for operational processes, records, and member voting; to establish technical security, encryption, and cryptographic standards including quantum-safe encryption and private key issuance; to provide for retained data access rights for former homeowners; to place caps on punitive fines for minor covenant violations; to authorize self-help remediation and define allowable fee recovery; to mandate decennial revisions of governing documents and allow temporary quorum rule suspensions; to revise quorum calculation rules by excluding corporate-owned properties; to provide for related matters; to provide for an effective date; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:

**SECTION 1\.** This Act shall be known and may be cited as the "Georgia Homeowner's Freedom Act of 2027."

**SECTION 2\.** Article 6 of Chapter 3 of Title 44 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to property owners' associations, is amended by adding a new Code section to read as follows:

"44-3-231.1. (a) On or before January 1, 2028, every property owners' association subject to this article shall establish, maintain, and provide all lot owners continuous, electronic access to a dedicated online portal. The online portal shall serve as a centralized system for association operations, including, but not limited to: (1) Access to detailed board meeting minutes and schedules of upcoming meetings; (2) Electronic voting and tallying for association matters; (3) Inspection and management of assessed fines, fees, and accounting balances; (4) Reservation and rental scheduling for community clubhouses and shared amenities; and (5) Submission, tracking, and approval processing for major architectural property changes, including exterior paint colors, tree removals, and structural modifications.

(b) The online portal required under subsection (a) of this Code section shall adhere to the following technical and security requirements: (1) All communications transmitted to or from the portal shall be secured using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols; (2) All data stored within or utilized by the portal shall be encrypted at rest using industry-accepted standard encryption algorithms; (3) All portal communications, data transmissions, and cryptographic primitives shall be secured utilizing post-quantum, quantum-safe cryptographic algorithms; and (4) Voting mechanisms hosted on the portal shall maintain fully auditable digital records ensuring voter verification without compromising voting secrecy.

(c) Each lot owner shall be issued a unique private key generated specifically for that owner. The private key shall be utilized by the lot owner to encrypt personal identifiable data and electronic voting records maintained within the portal.

(d) Upon the sale, transfer, or conveyance of a lot within the development, the former lot owner shall retain read-only access to all of their personal portal data, account history, and voting records for a period of no less than three years from the date of conveyance."

**SECTION 3\.** Code Section 44-3-223 of said article, relating to compliance with provisions of instrument, rules, and regulations, and penalties for noncompliance, is amended by revising subsection (a) as follows:

"(a) Every lot owner and all those entitled to occupy a lot shall comply with all lawful provisions of this article and of the property owners' association instruments. Disregard of the same shall be grounds for an action to recover sums due, for damages or injunctive relief, or both, maintainable by the association or, in a proper case, by an aggrieved lot owner.

(b)(1) To the extent provided in the instrument, the association shall have the power to impose reasonable fines for violations of the declaration, bylaws, or rules and regulations. However, for minor or non-safety-related administrative violations—including, but not limited to, delayed removal of waste receptacles or minor land maintenance oversights—punitive fines shall not exceed a cumulative total cap of $100.00 per violation occurrence, regardless of whether such fine is calculated on a daily or recurring schedule.

(2) In lieu of or in addition to imposing fines for noncompliance, the association may exercise self-help remediation by entry upon a lot to cure or abate a violation, provided that the lot owner is given at least seven business days' written notice. The association may bill the lot owner for the actual, direct costs incurred in remediating the violation, plus an administrative fee or fine not to exceed $100.00 total. Such remediated costs shall constitute a assessment against the lot."

**SECTION 4\.** Code Section 44-3-226 of said article, relating to the amendment of instruments, is amended by adding a new subsection to read as follows:

"(d)(1) Every ten years following the recording of the initial declaration or the effective date of this Act, whichever is later, the board of directors shall review and revise all governing documents, including the declaration, bylaws, and articles of incorporation, to ensure alignment with current state law and community needs.

(2) For the sole purpose of adopting the decennial revisions described in paragraph (1) of this subsection, the board of directors may, by unanimous vote of the board, temporarily suspend standard quorum requirements to submit the revised governing documents to the membership for ratification."

**SECTION 5\.** Code Section 44-3-228 of said article, relating to the presence of quorums at meetings, is amended as follows:

"44-3-228. (a) Unless the instrument or bylaws provide otherwise, a quorum shall be deemed present throughout any meeting of the association if persons entitled to cast more than one-third of the votes are present in person or by proxy at the beginning of the meeting.

(b) For the purpose of establishing or calculating a quorum at any annual or special meeting of the association, any lot owned by a corporation, limited liability company, partnership, real estate investment trust, or other legal business entity shall be excluded from the total number of eligible lot votes used to compute the quorum requirement.

*(Example: In a development comprising 500 total lots, if 100 lots are titled to corporate or business entities, the baseline total for quorum calculation shall be reduced to 400 lots, requiring a minimum of 134 lot owner votes to attain a one-third quorum).* "

**SECTION 6\.** This Act shall become effective on July 1, 2027, upon its approval by the Governor or upon its becoming law without such approval.

**SECTION 7\.** All laws and parts of laws in conflict with this Act are repealed.
